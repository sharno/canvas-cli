# Outcomes and Rubrics

## Scope
Learning outcomes and rubric management.

## Tasks
- Implement `outcome list/create/update/delete`.
- Implement `rubric list/create/update/delete`.
- Attach rubrics to assignments and outcomes.
- Support rubric-based grading selections.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Outcomes and rubrics validate required fields at edge.
- Attach/detach actions require `--confirm`.
- JSON output includes IDs and criteria summaries.

## Notes
- Keep rubric selection types explicit to avoid invalid states.
